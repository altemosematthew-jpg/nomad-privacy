Pocket Terminal Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email altemosematthew@gmail.com with your device model and iOS version.

Prices look stale? Stock quotes from free sources are delayed. Pull down on any screen to refresh, and check Settings → Data for the refresh interval.

Switch between Simple and Terminal? Tap the button in the top-left corner of any tab.

Didn't get a price alert? Alerts are checked while the app is open. Make sure notifications are allowed in Settings → Notifications → Pocket Terminal.

Where is my data? Only on your device. See the privacy policy.
