Pocket Terminal Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026 · Developer: Matthew Altemose · Contact: altemosematthew@gmail.com

Pocket Terminal is a market information app. This policy explains what it does with your information.

We don't collect your data

Pocket Terminal has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your watchlists, holdings, notes, journal entries, price alerts and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. You can export them yourself from Settings.

Market data requests

To show prices, charts, news and filings, the app requests public data directly from third-party sources: Yahoo Finance, Coinbase, the U.S. SEC (EDGAR), and financial news publishers (CNBC, Nasdaq, Seeking Alpha, Investing.com). These requests contain the ticker symbols being looked up and, like any internet request, your device's IP address. They are handled under those providers' own privacy policies. Pocket Terminal does not send them your watchlists, holdings, notes or journal.

Notifications

If you set a price alert, the app asks permission to show notifications. Alerts are checked on your device.

Children

Pocket Terminal is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Not investment advice

Pocket Terminal is for information and education only. Data is delayed and may be inaccurate. The app never places trades or connects to a brokerage.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released.
