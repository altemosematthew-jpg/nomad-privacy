How the numbers are computed, and what "best" means in each lens
vs fair
Cboe's theoretical value (its own volatility-surface price) compared with what you'd pay (the ask) or receive (the bid). +4% means the ask sits 4% under theo. The single most direct "is this contract cheap" number.
P(profit)
Black–Scholes probability the stock finishes beyond your breakeven at expiry, using the contract's own implied volatility. For sellers it's the chance the option expires worthless or below the credit you took.
IV vs 30d
The contract's implied volatility relative to the symbol's 30-day at-the-money IV. Negative = this strike is priced calmer than the rest of the chain (cheaper vol).
Theta/day
Daily time decay as a percent of the option's mid price. 1.5% means the premium bleeds 1.5% a day if nothing moves. Buyers want low; sellers want high.
Spread
(ask − bid) ÷ mid. Anything over ~10% is expensive to get in and out of; the score punishes it hard.
Score
Percentile ranks blended per lens (weights shown in the lens description). Only contracts that pass your filters are ranked, so the score is relative to the current view.
Leverage
|Δ| × stock price ÷ option price: how many dollars of stock exposure each dollar of premium controls.
⚡ Earnings / event
The contract expires after a scheduled earnings report (dates from Robinhood when loaded there, otherwise a built-in calendar), or the chain itself says an event is coming: front-month at-the-money IV sits well above later months, and the gap converts to an implied event move (±%). Buying through an event is a bet on a move bigger than that number, and IV usually collapses right after the report.
My positions
Loads your open option positions from Robinhood (in your browser, with your login) and reads each one: P/L, the move still needed to reach your cost-basis breakeven versus the market's own one-standard-deviation move by expiry, time decay per day, chance of profit at today's price and from your cost, and whether earnings fall inside the window. The label (On track / Fair bet / Needs a move / Long shot) follows Robinhood's chance-of-profit bands; it is a reading of the odds, not a recommendation.
Data
Cboe delayed quotes (about 15 minutes behind the market) from the live server, with a built-in snapshot as fallback; Robinhood quotes are real-time. Greeks and theo are the source's. Nothing here is advice; it is a ranked list of what the chain says right now.

Not investment advice. Options can lose their entire premium; short options can lose more than the premium received. Cboe quotes are delayed ~15 minutes (Robinhood mode is real-time); confirm the live price inside Robinhood before you submit an order.
