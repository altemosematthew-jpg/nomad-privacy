        What is Mapelago?

        Mapelago is a free daily geography game. Slide the tiles to merge them. Each new tile size earns a clue about today's mystery city. When you think you know it, drop a pin on the map. You get three pins, and everyone in the world gets the same city.

        Find today's mystery city

        1. Everyone gets the same city today.
        2. Slide the tiles. Two matching tiles join into one. The first time you make each of these tiles, you get a clue:
          1. Each move costs 8 points, so drop a pin as soon as you think you know.
          2. Land within 50 km and you've found it. A miss costs 150 points and tells you how far off you are and which way to go. You have three pins.
          3. If the board jams, you have to pin. A new city arrives at midnight. Mondays are easier than Sundays.
