Mapelago

Privacy

Mapelago has no accounts, no ads, no cookies and no tracking scripts. This page describes everything the site does with data.

Last updated November 22, 2026.

What is saved, and where

Your game in progress, your past results, your streak, the look you picked and whether you've seen the help screen are saved in your browser's local storage, on your own device. That information is never sent anywhere. Clearing this site's data in your browser erases it.

After your first visit, your browser also keeps a copy of the game's own files, so it opens quickly and works without a connection.

What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you choose to share. When you tap a share button, your device's own share sheet or clipboard gets a short text or an image. A challenge link holds three numbers: the puzzle number, your score and how many pins you used.

The game makes no requests to other companies. The map is built into the page, and the fonts come from this site itself.

The web host

The site's files are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details, such as your IP address, when your browser loads a page. GitHub's privacy statement explains what it does with them.

Children

Mapelago is made for a general audience. It doesn't ask anyone for personal information.

Changes

If the game ever adds accounts, leaderboards or visit counting, this page will say so before the change goes live, and the date above will change.
