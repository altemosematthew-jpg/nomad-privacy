        What is Voyage?
        
    
        Voyage is the daily trivia trip on Mapelago. Ten islands, one question on each: capitals, country outlines, borders, a mark on the globe, sizes and languages. Each island is worth more than the last, up to 1,000 points.
        
    
        After every right answer you choose. Drop anchor and keep your points, or sail on. A wrong answer ends the trip and leaves you with the points from the last harbor, at islands 3 and 6. You get one spyglass a day. Everyone gets the same ten questions.
