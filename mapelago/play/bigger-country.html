      What is Bigger country?
      
    
      Bigger country? is a one-minute daily game on Mapelago. You get ten pairs of countries and tap the one that covers more land. The pairs start far apart and get closer. Everyone in the world gets the same ten pairs, and new ones arrive at midnight, your time.
      
    
      Areas are measured from the map's own outlines, so they are rounded and include a country's islands and overseas regions.
