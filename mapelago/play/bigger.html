      What is Bigger city?
      
    
      Bigger city? is a one-minute daily game on Mapelago. You get ten pairs of cities and tap the one where more people live. The pairs start far apart and get closer. Everyone in the world gets the same ten pairs, and new ones arrive at midnight, your time.
      
    
      The numbers count people in the city itself, from GeoNames. A famous city with big suburbs can be smaller than you expect.
