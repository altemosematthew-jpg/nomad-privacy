NOMAD Privacy Policy

Effective: September 27, 2026 · Developer: Matthew Altemose · Contact: altemosematthew@gmail.com

NOMAD is a location-based exploration game. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What stays on your device. NOMAD 1.0 has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no advertising. Your passport, discoveries, photos, spots you found and settings are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Location. NOMAD uses your location while the app is open to show nearby spots and to check that you are really at a spot when you discover it. Your location is processed on your device and is not sent to us. Nearby places are looked up through Apple Maps, which Apple handles under Apple's privacy policy.

Camera and photos. NOMAD uses the camera only when you take a discovery or founding photo. Photos are saved inside the app on your device and are not uploaded.

Tracking. NOMAD does not track you across apps or websites and does not share data with third parties.

Children. NOMAD is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes. If a future version adds online features (accounts, sharing photos, leaderboards), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app will ask for any new permissions.
