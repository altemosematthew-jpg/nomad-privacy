Studyvale Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

Studyvale is a study app for iPhone, iPad and Mac, made by ALTEMOSE. It is built so that we never see your data.

What we collect

Nothing. Studyvale has no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. Your notes, flashcards, progress, mood check-ins and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

Network use

The app works fully offline. The only time anything leaves your device is if you choose "Send suggestion" in the Library, which sends the subject name and cards you typed to a review queue so they can be added for everyone. Nothing identifies you, and every other feature works with no connection.

Children

Studyvale is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. A built-in word filter blocks offensive language in anything typed.

Your data

Delete the app, or use "Erase everything on this device" in Settings, and your data is gone. There is nothing to request from us, because we hold nothing.
