Studyvale Support

Studyvale is a free, offline study valley for every age: notes, spaced-repetition flashcards, quizzes, teach-it-back, a focus timer and a growing subject library.

Common questions

Does it cost anything? No. Studyvale is free, with no ads and no account.

Does it work offline? Yes, fully. Your data stays on your device.

How do I make my own deck? In Notes, write lines as term: meaning, then tap "Make flashcards".

How do I change how it looks? Settings > Season & vibe. You can also turn animations off.

Who is studying? Settings lets you pick an age band, which adjusts text size and timer lengths.
