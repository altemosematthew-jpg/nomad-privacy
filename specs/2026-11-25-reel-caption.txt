New US tariffs on 16 economies are close, Reuters and AP report. China and the EU are on the list.

The tariffs would follow a US probe into excess factory capacity that opened in March. A source told Reuters that caps from earlier trade deals would be kept. That means 15% for the EU, Japan and South Korea. By early Wednesday the White House had not commented and no official rates were out. Beijing called overcapacity "a false issue".

The Nasdaq 100 fund (QQQ) fell 2.0% on Tuesday, led by chip stocks. Nasdaq futures lost over 2% more early Wednesday, and Reuters gives the tariff plan as one reason.
