One deal in Abu Dhabi | Traders still price peace at 10%

Two days of US-run talks between Russia and Ukraine ended Wednesday with one agreement. Watch to the end.

What happened:
- Russia and Ukraine agreed to a large prisoner exchange. US envoy Jared Kushner called it "the biggest humanitarian release of prisoners." Russia says it is agreed in principle.
- A Russian officer told state TV the swap could happen in two to three weeks.
- It was the first meeting of all three sides in nine months.

What they didn't get:
- No ceasefire. Kyiv had hoped for one at sea and on energy targets.
- Territory, security guarantees and Europe's role are still open. Kushner says there is "no trust right now between the sides."
- Strikes went on during the talks, including a Russian attack on a market in the Odesa region and a fire at Russia's Ust-Luga oil port after a drone attack.

The market:
- On Polymarket, a prediction market, traders priced a ceasefire by 31 December at about 10% on Wednesday, and by the end of 2027 at about 47%.

The catch:
- Security advisers from France, Germany, the UK and Italy were in Abu Dhabi but met Ukraine separately. Zelenskyy says Europe must be represented at every stage. The Kremlin says Europeans could join later.

Coming up:
- US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner are due to meet Putin in Moscow on Thursday, then Zelenskyy in Dublin.
