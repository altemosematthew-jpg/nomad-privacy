Trump, Xi and Putin sit down together on Thursday, for the first time ever.

They meet in Shenzhen at 2:30 p.m. local time. Between them they hold more than 10,000 nuclear warheads, and the last US-Russia arms treaty expired in February. Trump wants a new deal that includes China. Beijing has said the two bigger arsenals should shrink first.

US officials say they don't expect an agreement. One called it "the first conversation." China's foreign ministry says the meeting has "groundbreaking significance." Ukraine won't be at the table.
