Two Russian missiles crashed in Poland on Friday, by Warsaw's count, one of them about 35 km from a drill by 11 allied countries.

No one was hurt. Polish prosecutors say one was a Kh-101 cruise missile made in the last quarter of this year. Prime Minister Donald Tusk called it an "intentional provocation." Hours earlier, Trump had told Fox News that Russian drones and missiles were landing in Poland "by mistake." As of Saturday, Moscow had not commented. NATO's chief went to Warsaw, and the alliance's council meets on Monday.

EPOL, a US-listed fund of Polish stocks, fell 4.9% on Friday. The S&P 500 fell 3.7% the same day.
