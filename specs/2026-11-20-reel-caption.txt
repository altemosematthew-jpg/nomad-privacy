Trump, Xi and Putin pledged more fuel for the world on Thursday.

It is one of four points in the declaration they released after their first three-way meeting, in Shenzhen. The text says the three countries "plan to increase" the supply of refined fuel. It gives no volumes and no dates, and it never mentions Ukraine or Iran.

Trump said they were "making history." Germany's chancellor called the meeting a "photo op," RFE/RL reports. Rystad Energy says the pledge offers limited near-term relief. The Brent oil fund (BNO) closed up 1.4% on the day.
