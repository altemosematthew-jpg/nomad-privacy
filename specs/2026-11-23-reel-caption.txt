Taiwan's stock market fell 17.5% in a week, and on Monday it tightened its limits on short selling.

It was the Taiex index's worst week in 35 years, Bloomberg reports. From Monday, no listed stock can be sold short below its previous close. A state fund of about $15 billion is buying shares. South Korea put a fund on standby, and China's exchanges will ease a margin rule. Taiwan's premier says the economy's fundamentals are solid. Bloomberg notes that two earlier rounds of curbs this month did not steady the market. On day one the Taiex rose 2.2%, while Seoul's Kospi fell 3.3%.

EWT, a US-listed fund of Taiwan stocks, lost 23% last week.
