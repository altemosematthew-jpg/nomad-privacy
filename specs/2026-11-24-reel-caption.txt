Ukraine says its sea drones sank a Russian warship in Libya, about 2,400 km from the front line.

Zelenskyy says seven sea drones were used on Sunday against the corvette Merkury, docked at Benghazi's naval base. Satellite images show the ship capsized. As of Monday night, Moscow had not confirmed the loss, and the Kremlin referred questions to its Defense Ministry. Libya's eastern government called the strike a violation of sovereignty and asked the UN to investigate.

Oil prices did not rise. BNO, a US-listed Brent oil fund, fell 4.0% on Monday. Reuters cites more supply from the Middle East and a Libyan pipeline that restarted the same day.
