The US just made it harder for Chinese AI firms to rent American chips abroad.

On Monday, Commerce said advanced AI chips headed to data centers in Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia and Brazil will need a US license. Data centers already serving Chinese companies get 90 days.

Beijing called it "typical economic coercion" and says it will hit back if the US keeps going. The rule itself calls it a "logical extension of existing controls."

Chip stocks (the SMH fund) fell 5.1% the day it landed.
