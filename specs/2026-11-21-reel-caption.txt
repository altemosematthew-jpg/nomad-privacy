Three missiles were fired at Riyadh early Saturday, and the Houthis are telling ships to leave Saudi ports.

The Saudi-led coalition says it shot down all three. The Houthis say they are striking targets across the kingdom. On Friday the coalition struck Yemen's Hodeidah port, saying the port was used to smuggle weapons. The Houthis answered by threatening Jeddah and every Saudi port on the Red Sea. That coast is where Saudi Arabia's oil pipeline around the Strait of Hormuz ends.

The Brent oil fund (BNO) rose 0.3% on Friday and still lost 3.3% for the week, its third weekly drop in a row. Analysts told Reuters that Gulf exports stayed strong.
