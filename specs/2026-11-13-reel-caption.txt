Nvidia's CEO is out | What changes now

Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia's CEO on Thursday, effective immediately. Watch to the end.

What happened:
- Huang stepped down as president, CEO and director. Finance chief Colette Kress is interim CEO.
- Harvey Jones was named the first board chair in Nvidia's history.
- The board is searching for a permanent CEO and will look at internal and external candidates.

Why it matters:
- On November 8 the board opened an investigation into reports that Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort, AP reported.

The market:
- Nvidia hit a 52-week low of $148.80 during Thursday's session and closed at $166.19, down 4.3%.
- Rivals rose: AMD +5.9%, Broadcom +5.5%.

Coming up:
- Nvidia reports earnings on November 17.
