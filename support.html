NOMAD Support

Need help with NOMAD, found a bug, or have an idea? Email altemosematthew@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Spots won't discover? You need to be standing close to the spot. Make sure Location is allowed for NOMAD in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services.

Camera not working? Allow Camera access for NOMAD in Settings > NOMAD.

Where is my data? Everything is stored on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
